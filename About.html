About the Academy

Where Tradition Meets Passion

Uthan Tabla Academy is dedicated to the teaching, practice and promotion of tabla and Indian classical music. Since 1998, we have built a home in Varanasi for students who want to learn this instrument properly, from the ground up.

The academy provides students with a disciplined and encouraging environment where they can understand the fundamentals of tabla, develop their musical abilities, and experience the rich tradition of Indian classical music. Courses run across three levels — beginner, intermediate and advanced — supported by our quiz platform, so students can track their understanding as they progress.

Through regular practice, personal guidance and performance opportunities, students are encouraged to grow both as musicians and as individuals.

Shri Kartik Bhalerao

Tabla Artist & Teacher

Shri Kartik Bhalerao is a tabla artist and faculty member at Uthan Tabla Academy, guiding students in the fundamentals and practical aspects of tabla.

His teaching focuses on building a strong understanding of taal, laya, technique and disciplined riyaz, drawn from his own years of training and performance.

8+ years teaching Gold Medalist, Pune University MA in Tabla
